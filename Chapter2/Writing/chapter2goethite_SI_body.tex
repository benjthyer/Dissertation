\subsection*{Permeation Step Method Step-Heat Dataset}\label{ch2-si-distilled-permstep}

The complete corrected step-heat dataset produced by the permeation step
method, using the in-experiment rate constants above
(Fig.~\ref{fig:ch2-ampoule-permeability}), is reported in
Table~\ref{tab:ch2-distilled-permstep-3he}, giving the central value and
2$\sigma$ uncertainty for each quantity, analogous to
Table~\ref{tab:ch2-distilled-3he} in the main text for the bounding method.
4He is not treated by the permeation step method here: 4He
permeation-step signals were blank level,
so no direct, step-by-step quantification of the ampoule's 4He inventory was
possible, and 4He throughout this chapter is instead reduced exclusively by
the bounding method of the main text (\S\ref{ch2-analytical-methods}). The
permeation step dataset reported here is retained only for 3He,
as the internal check on the bounding method discussed in
\S\ref{complete-degas-test}.

\subsection{Permeation Step Approach and Concordance with Bounding Approach}\label{si:permlag-concordance}

Beyond the ampoule's residual helium content itself (\S\ref{complete-degas-test}), the two methods' derived apparent diffusivities agree just as closely. Fig.~\ref{fig:ch2-diffusivity-comparison} compares $\ln(D/a^2)$ from the permeation-step method against the bounding method's central value for every step in the experiment, with the bounding method's own lower- and upper-bound spread on one axis and the permeation step method's propagated uncertainty on the other; the two steps marked ``$^\dagger$'' in Table~\ref{tab:ch2-distilled-permstep-3he} above are excluded for the reason given there. The two diffusivity datasets track the 1:1 line within uncertainty across the full range of the experiment. Fitting the permeation-step method's own diffusivities with the same Stage 1 procedure used for the bounding method in the main text (Fig.~\ref{fig:ch2-permstep-arrhenius-zoom}) yields an activation energy of \ChTwoPermStepEa$\pm$\ChTwoPermStepEaErr\ kJ/mol (R\textsuperscript{2}=\ChTwoPermStepRtwo, n=\ChTwoPermStepN) and a closure temperature of \ChTwoPermStepTc$\pm$\ChTwoPermStepTcErr\textdegree C, indistinguishable from the bounding method's own \ChTwoEa$\pm$\ChTwoEaErr\ kJ/mol and \ChTwoTc$\pm$\ChTwoTcErr\textdegree C (Fig.~\ref{fig:ch2-arrhenius-zoom}, main text).
